Tappadoo Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Tappadoo is a mobile game published by Wopa software ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game handles and what it does not.

In short

  • You do not need an account to play, and the game never asks you for your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, microphone or camera.
  • Your progress is saved on your device only. We do not receive it.
  • The game shows no adverts and has no advertising software in it.
  • If you buy coins, the payment is handled by Google Play. The purchasing software in the game, made by Unity, collects some information to make purchases work; it is listed below.

Information kept on your device

The game saves your progress on your device: the levels you have won, your Crystals, the repairs you have made, your coins and Hammers, how many coins you have bought in total, and your settings (language, sound, music, vibration, calm). This stays on your device. We do not receive it. It is removed when you delete the game's data or uninstall the game.

Purchases

You can play Tappadoo without buying anything. If you choose to buy a pack of coins, the purchase is made through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment under its own privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). We never see or store your card details, your Google account password or your billing address.

To deliver what you bought, the game receives from Google Play a confirmation that a purchase was made and which pack it was. The game uses this on your device to add the coins. The game does not keep the order number, the price or the date. As the seller, we can see order records in our Google Play account, as every seller can (order number, product, price, country, date), and we use them only for support, refunds, accounting and tax as the law requires.

Software from other companies in the game

The game includes Unity In-App Purchasing and Unity Gaming Services Core, made by Unity Technologies, which the game uses only to connect to Google Play Billing. According to Unity, this software collects the following information and sends it to Unity, which uses it to make purchases work and for its own analytics of the service. Unity does not share it with other companies for their own use, and the information is encrypted when it is sent.

  • Approximate location (for example, the country worked out from the device's internet address)
  • Email address, where it is part of the purchase information
  • Personal identifiers and device or other identifiers, such as a random identifier for the installation of the game
  • Purchase history: which packs were bought
  • Crash logs, diagnostics and other app performance data, such as the device model, the operating system version and the app version

We do not receive this information. Unity's privacy policy, and how to ask Unity about the information it holds, is at https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.

Children

Tappadoo is intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help.

Your choices and rights

  • You can erase your progress at any time with "Start over" in Settings, or by deleting the game's data in your device's settings.
  • For purchases, refunds and your Google account data, use Google Play's own tools.
  • For the information Unity's purchasing software collects, use the request options in Unity's privacy policy.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information and to object to its use. We hold no information about you beyond the order records described above; write to us at the address below and we will help, including with a request to Unity.

Changes

If the game starts to handle information differently, for example if adverts or usage statistics are added, we will update this policy before that version is released and change the date above.
